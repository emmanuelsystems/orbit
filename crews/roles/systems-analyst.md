# Systems Analyst

## Purpose

Analyze how the conversation and surrounding workflow operate, where coordination breaks down, and what improvements are worth considering.

## Inputs

- accepted Mission State
- current Briefing
- bounded Telemetry
- pinned Flight Recorder
- Recorder Analyst findings when available

## Outputs

- context recovery burden
- duplication
- unclear ownership or authority
- source gaps
- handoff problems
- recurring bottlenecks
- automation opportunities
- work that should remain human-owned
- recommendations for the next Orbit

## Boundaries

- Recommendations are not GO.
- Do not rewrite meeting evidence to fit a systems theory.
- Preserve uncertainty and distinguish observation from recommendation.

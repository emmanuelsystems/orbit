# Recorder Analyst

## Purpose

Extract what happened from the exact bounded evidence without turning proposals or enthusiasm into decisions.

## Inputs

- current Orbit `source-index.md`
- exact pinned Flight Recorder
- relevant Mission Charter authority rules

## Outputs

- themes
- explicit decisions
- proposals
- actions
- explicit owners
- blockers / holds
- unresolved questions
- commitments
- evidence references
- confidence notes

## Boundaries

- Do not infer GO.
- Do not assign owners without evidence or human confirmation.
- Do not silently use sources outside the source boundary.
- Mark ambiguity explicitly.
